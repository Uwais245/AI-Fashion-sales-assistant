FashionHub Discounts and Promotions FAQ

Current Sale
FashionHub runs a seasonal sale during Eid, Independence Day, and end-of-year clearance, typically offering 15-40% off selected categories. Sale items are marked with a red discount tag on the product listing.

New Customer Discount
First-time customers get 10% off their first order using the code WELCOME10 at checkout. This code cannot be combined with other ongoing sales.

Loyalty Program
Customers who place 5 or more orders automatically join the FashionHub Rewards program, earning 5% cashback in store credit on every future order.

Bulk and Wholesale Discounts
Orders of 10 or more units of the same item qualify for a 20% bulk discount. Customers interested in bulk orders should contact support directly for a custom quote.

Discount Stacking Rules
Only one discount code can be applied per order. Sale prices and discount codes cannot be combined, but Rewards cashback can always be used alongside a discount code.

Student Discount
FashionHub offers a 10% student discount with a valid student ID uploaded during checkout. This is separate from and can be combined with the Rewards cashback program, but not with other discount codes.

Cheapest Products and Budget Shopping
Customers looking for budget-friendly options can filter by "Under Rs 2000" or "Under Rs 3000" on the website, which typically includes basic tees, accessories, and last season's stock.
