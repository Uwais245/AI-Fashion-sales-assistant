FashionHub Shipping and Delivery Policy

Delivery Timeframes
Orders within Lahore are delivered within 1-2 business days. Orders to Karachi, Islamabad, and other major cities are delivered within 3-4 business days. Orders to smaller towns and remote areas may take 5-7 business days.

Delivery Charges
Standard delivery costs Rs 200 for orders under Rs 3,000. Orders above Rs 3,000 qualify for free standard delivery. Same-day delivery is available only within Lahore for an additional Rs 350, and orders must be placed before 12:00 PM to qualify.

Order Tracking
Once an order is shipped, customers receive a tracking number via SMS and WhatsApp. Tracking status can also be checked by messaging our support team with the order ID.

Cash on Delivery (COD)
COD is available on all orders under Rs 15,000. Orders above this amount require prepayment via card, bank transfer, or mobile wallet.

Failed Delivery Attempts
Our courier partners attempt delivery up to 2 times. If both attempts fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and the customer is contacted to reschedule or cancel.

International Shipping
FashionHub currently ships only within Pakistan. International shipping is not yet supported.

Packaging
All orders are shipped in tamper-proof, branded packaging. Fragile items such as jewelry and eyewear include extra protective padding.
