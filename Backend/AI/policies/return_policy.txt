FashionHub Return Policy

Return Window
Items can be returned within 14 days of the delivery date. The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags attached. Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

How to Initiate a Return
Customers can request a return by messaging our support team with their order ID and the reason for return. Once approved, we will share a return pickup address or a courier drop-off code depending on the customer's city.

Refund Process
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are issued via bank transfer or store credit, whichever the customer prefers.

Damaged or Incorrect Items
If a customer receives a damaged item or the wrong product, they should share a photo of the item along with their order ID within 48 hours of delivery. In these cases, FashionHub covers the full cost of return shipping and offers either a replacement or a full refund, no questions asked.

Non-Returnable Items
The following items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons: innerwear, swimwear, and earrings. Custom or made-to-order items are also non-returnable unless defective.

Store Credit Option
Customers who prefer store credit instead of a refund receive an additional 10% bonus credit as a thank-you for choosing store credit.
