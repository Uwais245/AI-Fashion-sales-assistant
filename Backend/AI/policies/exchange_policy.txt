FashionHub Exchange Policy

Exchange Window
Customers can request a size or color exchange within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unworn, unwashed, with tags still attached.

How Exchanges Work
Once an exchange is approved, FashionHub ships the new size or color first and arranges pickup of the original item at the same time, so customers are not left without the product for long. If the requested replacement is out of stock, we offer a refund or store credit instead.

Exchange Eligibility
Only items of equal or lower price can be exchanged directly. If a customer wants to exchange for a higher-priced item, they will need to pay the price difference before the new item ships.

One-Time Exchange Limit
Each order is eligible for one free exchange. Additional exchanges on the same order may incur a Rs 150 handling fee.

Non-Exchangeable Items
Innerwear, swimwear, earrings, and Final Sale items are not eligible for exchange, for the same hygiene and clearance reasons they are non-returnable.

Exchange for Damaged Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, the one-time exchange limit and handling fee do not apply. Customers can request unlimited free exchanges in these cases until they receive a satisfactory replacement.
