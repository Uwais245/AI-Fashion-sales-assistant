FashionHub Size Guide

Women's Clothing Sizes
Small (S): Bust 34", Waist 28", Hips 37"
Medium (M): Bust 36", Waist 30", Hips 39"
Large (L): Bust 38", Waist 32", Hips 41"
Extra Large (XL): Bust 40", Waist 34", Hips 43"

Men's Clothing Sizes
Small (S): Chest 36", Waist 30"
Medium (M): Chest 38", Waist 32"
Large (L): Chest 40", Waist 34"
Extra Large (XL): Chest 42", Waist 36"

How to Measure Yourself
Bust/Chest: Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground.
Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of the natural waistline.
Hips: Measure around the fullest part of the hips.

Fit Notes
Our dresses generally run true to size. Our formal shirts run slightly narrow in the shoulders, so customers between sizes are advised to size up. Our jeans and casual wear have a relaxed fit and run slightly large.

Size Availability
Most products are available in S, M, L, and XL. Selected bestsellers also offer XS and XXL. Size availability varies by product and can be confirmed by checking the specific product's stock listing.

Choosing the Right Size
If a customer is unsure which size to order, we recommend comparing their own measurements to the size chart above rather than relying on their usual size from other brands, since fit varies between brands.
